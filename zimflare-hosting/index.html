Frequently Asked Questions

What is web hosting? +
Web hosting is a service that allows you to publish a website on the internet. When you sign up for hosting, we store your website files on our secure servers so people can visit your site 24/7. Think of it like renting space on the internet for your business.
What is a domain name? +
A domain name is your website's address on the internet (like zimflare.co.zw). It's what people type into their browser to visit your site. We help you register your domain and connect it to your hosting account.
Can I register a .co.zw domain? +
Yes! We specialize in .co.zw domains for Zimbabwe-based businesses. We also offer .org.zw for non-profits, .ac.zw for academic institutions, .com, .africa, and many more extensions. Register your domain and start building your online presence today.
Do I need a website before buying hosting? +
Not at all! Most of our customers start with hosting and build their website after. With our 1-click WordPress install, you can have a website running within minutes. We also offer free website design with annual plans if you need help getting started.
Can you help me migrate my website? +
Absolutely! If you're currently hosted elsewhere, we offer free migration for Scale Plan customers. Our technical team will handle everything so you don't have to worry about downtime. Just contact us and we'll take care of it.
Do you support WordPress? +
Yes! WordPress is our passion. Our hosting is fully optimized for WordPress with 1-click install, LiteSpeed caching for speed, and automatic updates. We also have WordPress experts available via WhatsApp and email if you need help.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept international credit cards, PayPal, and are adding more local Zimbabwe payment methods. Contact us on WhatsApp to discuss payment options that work best for you.
Can I upgrade my hosting plan anytime? +
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime. If you upgrade, we'll refund the unused portion of your current plan and charge the difference. It's easy and hassle-free.
